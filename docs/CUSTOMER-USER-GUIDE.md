# Customer User Guide

For shoppers. It walks through finding a product, buying it and looking after your order.

---

## 1. Finding your way around

1. The **header** has the logo, the search icon, your account, the wishlist heart and the cart. Under it is the category bar.
2. The **About** menu explains how Rewire inspects and grades devices, and has the FAQ.
3. The **footer** links to Warranty, Shipping, Returns, Terms, Privacy and Support.
4. On a phone, a bottom bar gives quick access to the main pages.

## 2. Creating an account

You can browse without one. An account lets you save a wishlist, use promo codes and see all your orders.

1. Select the account icon, then **Sign up**.
2. Enter your details and create a password.
3. Check your email and verify your address. Orders you placed earlier as a guest with the same email then appear in your account.

## 3. Browsing products

1. Pick a category from the category bar, or choose **Shop** to see everything.
2. Each card shows the product, its condition and price.
3. Use **Sort** to order by price or newest.
4. Use the page numbers under the grid to move through results. Your filters stay as you move.

## 4. Searching

1. Select the search icon in the header.
2. Type at least two letters. Suggestions appear as you type.
3. Pick a suggestion, or press Enter to see all results.
4. If nothing matches, you will see "No results found". Select **Clear search** to start again.

## 5. Filtering

On the shop page, use the filter panel on the left, or **Filters** on a phone.

1. **Availability.** In stock is ticked by default. Untick it to include out of stock items.
2. **Category, Condition, Grade, Brand and Storage.** Tick as many as you like.
3. **Price.** Set the range you want.
4. Active filters appear as chips. Remove one with its chip, or select **Clear all**.

## 6. Looking at a product

1. Select a product card.
2. Choose the **storage, colour and condition** you want. The price, photos and stock update.
3. Read the specifications, what is included, the warranty and the terms further down.
4. Tick any **add-ons** you want, such as a case, charger or extended warranty. Some add months of warranty, shown as "+12 months warranty".

## 7. Adding to the cart

1. Select **Add to cart**.
2. Use the quantity stepper to change how many you want.
3. Open the cart from the header at any time.
4. On the cart page you can change quantity, add or remove add-ons, or remove an item.
5. If a price changed or an item became unavailable, the cart tells you. Select **Got it** to accept a new price, and remove unavailable items before you continue.
6. Select **Proceed to checkout**.

Your cart is saved. If you add items as a guest and then sign in, they are merged into your account cart.

## 8. Wishlist

1. Select the **heart** on a product card or product page to save it.
2. Open the wishlist from the header or from your account.
3. Select the heart again to remove an item, or add it to the cart from the wishlist page.

## 9. Promo codes

1. Sign in. Promo codes need an account.
2. On the checkout summary, enter your code in the promo field and press Apply or Enter.
3. The discount shows in the summary. Select remove to take it off.
4. If the code is refused, the message tells you why: not active yet, expired, fully used, already used by you, not valid for these items, or a minimum spend is needed.

## 10. Checkout

Checkout has four steps. You move through them in order, and you can select **Edit** on a finished step to change it.

1. **Information.** Email, phone number and delivery address. Choose your emirate. If we deliver by area, pick your area from the list.
2. **Delivery.** Choose a delivery method. The fee and estimated time are shown.
3. **Payment.** Choose how to pay.
4. **Review.** Check your items, add-ons, promo discount, delivery fee, VAT and total, then select **Place Order**.

## 11. Payment

1. **Cash on delivery.** Your order is confirmed straight away and you pay when it arrives.
2. **Card or Apple Pay.** After you place the order you are taken to Stripe's secure page to pay. We never see your card details.
3. When payment goes through you return to Rewire and your order is confirmed.
4. If you leave the Stripe page, your order is saved but not confirmed. Pay within 30 minutes, or contact support.

## 12. After you order

1. You see an order confirmation page with your order number, which starts with RW.
2. A confirmation email is sent to you.
3. **Signed in:** open **Account → Orders** to follow progress and download your tax invoice.
4. **Guest:** go to **Track Order** and enter your order number and email.
5. Order steps are Placed, Confirmed, Processing, Dispatched and Delivered. To change or cancel an order, contact support before it is dispatched.

## 13. Warranty, returns and support

1. **Warranty.** Starts on delivery. Your order page shows "Warranty until" for each item.
2. **Returns.** Within the return window, open the delivered order in your account and select **Request a return**, or **Return this item** on one line. Track the request under **Account → Returns**.
3. **Support.** Select **Support** in the footer to read the FAQ or raise a ticket. Your tickets are under **Account → Tickets**.

## 14. Your account

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Orders | History, tracking and invoices |
| Wishlist | Saved products |
| Addresses | Saved delivery addresses |
| Returns | Return requests and refunds |
| Tickets | Support conversations |
| Waitlists | Releases you asked to be told about |
| Settings | Your details and password |

## 15. Forgot your password

1. Go to **Sign in**, then **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email and follow the link we send.
3. Choose a new password.
